Campaign Brief

INDISCREET is a beauty campaign centered on sensuality, confidence, and individuality. Inspired by the provocative glamour of early-2000s fashion editorials, the campaign brings together the warm, smoky character of Tom Ford Tobacco Vanille and the deep brick-red shade of Runway Lip Color in Invite Only.
