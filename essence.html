Essence

A promotional movie poster built from a hand sketch, developed with AI, then refined by hand in post.

Final Poster

The Process

Here's how it came together, from the first pen sketch to the poster above.

01 — Sketch

The Hand-Drawn Sketch

I started with a rough pen sketch: the title "ESSENCE" running vertically along the left edge, and a faceless woman with long dark hair holding a cat, cropped at the shoulders inside a simple bordered frame.

↓
02 — First AI Pass

Into Gemini

I fed the sketch into Gemini with a methodical breakdown of each element: the vertical bold "ESSENCE" title with a drop shadow, an aged cream paper texture, a halftone-shaded illustration of a woman with long black hair, her face left blank/featureless, cradling a small black cat, and placeholder credit text along the bottom in the style of a real movie poster.

"A vintage-style movie poster on aged cream paper. Vertical bold slab-serif text reading ESSENCE running down the left side with a soft drop shadow. On the right, a halftone-illustrated woman with long straight black hair, no facial features, shoulders bare, holding a small black cat against her chest. Thin hairline border around the whole poster. Credit text along the bottom in small caps."
↓
03 — Final Edit

The Finished Poster

The AI version was close, but not quite right — the face read as too "there" for what I wanted, and the bottom text was still just a placeholder. I brought it into an image editor and erased the leftover facial marks so the face is completely blank, then replaced the bottom credit line with the real tagline, "Coming Soon to Theaters." That gave me the final poster shown above.
